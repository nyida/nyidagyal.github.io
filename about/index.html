About SnakeGuide

A technical resource on state constraints, planning, and learning in Snake, maintained by Nyida Gyal.

Research questions

Snake exposes a compact set of difficult modeling choices. A body segment is an obstacle now and a free cell later. A shortest food route may destroy the next escape route. Two boards with identical occupancy may encode different body orders and therefore different futures.

The collection studies these questions through 18 expository notes, interactive figures, and a primary-source bibliography. It connects established methods to explicit Snake models.

Evidence and artifacts

The static-search experiment provides a fully specified generator, matched algorithm inputs, per-case data, and downloadable source. Its results measure graph search on a fixed finite suite. The laboratory provides inspectable demonstrations of game transitions and traversal.

Technical notes are explanatory syntheses, not peer-reviewed papers. Literature records identify the original authors and publication venues. Conceptual learning figures contain no measured training data. There is no trained-agent performance or competitive leaderboard claim in this edition.

Reference semantics

The simulator uses four-neighbor movement, solid boundaries, one food, immediate single-cell growth, and no starvation timer. A non-growing move may enter the cell vacated by the tail. The state model and engine note describe these choices. Other rulesets are discussed explicitly.
